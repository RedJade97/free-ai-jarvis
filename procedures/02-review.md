# Review and plan the next test

Purpose: produce a grounded edit/decision plan from an experiment.

1. Read the objective and predeclared success rule. Inventory all supplied variants and evidence IDs; identify missing inputs before analysis.
2. Match post age, platform and distribution. Keep retention, saves, shares and follows separate. Verify ratios against their paired counts.
3. Align only observed retention timestamps with the actual speech and screens. A drop is a location, not a causal explanation.
4. Examine comparable archive examples and counterexamples. Treat siblings as one concept. A small or biased archive should yield uncertainty.
5. Propose KEEP/CHANGE/CUT decisions with exact posted-cut intervals, reasons, editing versus new recording, and the metric to test. Preserve successful passages.
6. Write conclusion, limitations and next test; connect every proposal to evidence. Use hypothesis language for unproven changes.
7. Have the user verify the media and outcome. Save the review and transition only when its requirements are satisfied. Do not publish content or declare a winner without the user's decision.

Completion evidence: an evidence-linked review, coverage accounting, measurable next test and a recorded human decision. Unavailable data remains visible.
